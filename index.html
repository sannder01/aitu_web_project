Underground Gym in Astana

Underground Gym is a chain of fitness clubs in Astana. We created this website so that before your first visit, you can take your time exploring the gym facilities, the types of workouts available, and what to ask the receptionist. This is a fictional educational website: it is not connected to official bookings, and some descriptions were made up for the project.

The branch featured in this project

For this project, we chose the branch at 17 Zhanibek Tarkhan Street. The description covers the strength and cardio areas, free weights, and a space for functional training. You can plan a session for different goals: lift weights, add some cardio, or simply ease back into physical activity.

Before your first visit, it is useful to look at photos of the gym to get an idea of where the equipment and main areas are located. This helps you find your way around more quickly and avoid feeling lost in an unfamiliar place. That is why the project includes plans for photos to complement the branch description.

What you can find on this website

The workouts section brings together example memberships and brief explanations of the main types of training. There is no complicated terminology: just what you can expect to do and how the sessions differ.

On the trial workout page, you can view a sample questionnaire and think about your goals in advance. For example, you may want to build muscle, improve endurance, or simply move regularly after spending all day at a laptop. The form is a demonstration and does not send an application to a real club.

A separate page explains how the website was created, including its sections, file structure, and how the form works.

Before your visit

We recommend checking prices, opening hours, and membership conditions in advance, especially time restrictions, membership freezes, and trainer fees. It is also worth considering the journey: a conveniently located gym is easier to fit into your week. Bring a bottle, as all Underground branches provide water dispensers with cold and warm water. If you forget your bottle, you can always ask the receptionist for a cup.
