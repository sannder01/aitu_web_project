What the gym offers

The branch at 17 Tarkhan Street has several areas for different types of exercise. This branch also offers group sessions with a trainer. The gym includes the following areas:

  • Strength area
    • Exercise machines
    • Free weights
  • Cardio area
  • Functional training

Another reminder: it is useful to look at photos of the facilities before your first visit. This will help you picture the equipment layout and find your way around more quickly. If you have little training experience, you can ask whether an introduction to the machines is available.

Useful terms

Membership
The right to visit the club under agreed conditions.
Membership freeze

Before buying

It is worth contacting different branches and comparing their offers. You could also consider buying a membership from another person, after checking with the club whether an official transfer is possible. Check the remaining membership period and any restrictions. A lower price alone does not necessarily mean that the option suits your schedule and goals.
