Trial workout

On your first visit, I recommend consulting the club's staff or trainers before starting your workout. Before going to the gym, check the prices and opening hours, then book a trial session, preferably with a trainer.

How to prepare

  1. Call the branch that is convenient for you.
  2. Ask for the information you need, such as opening hours.
  3. Pack your bag with clean clothes and indoor shoes. If you plan to shower, also bring a towel, clean underwear, and socks.

If you have not visited this club before, ask about the changing rooms, gym rules, and how to exercise without injuring yourself. It is also useful to ask how much a workout costs with and without a trainer. Project address: 17 Zhanibek Tarkhan Street.

If calling is inconvenient, you can email info@undergroundgym.example - this is a demonstration address for the educational project. The club usually replies during the working day, but before your visit it is best to call anyway to confirm the time. For example, you could ask directly: Could you tell me when the gym is least crowded? P.S. Definitely not on weekend evenings. Then make a note of the receptionist's answer.
